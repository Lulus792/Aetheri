## Executed boundary

The separate512MiB GPT fixture boots through firmware's USB fallback path. Its
selected systemd initrd loads usb_storage/uas, imports block metadata through
udev, and runs the new C17 preflight before mounting the expected256MiB ext4
root read-only. Preflight verifies the actual block device, unique PARTUUID,
partition offset/size, parent size/diskseq/removable state, USB ancestry and
filesystem UUID. After mounting, it repeats the observation, checks actual
read-only ext4/device/profile, stops initrd udev and rechecks before standard
PID1 SwitchRoot. The original real-root diagnostic then verifies UEFI, mounted
root and EROFS, and powers off. Read the [contract](../../../spec/usb-root-probe.md).

This is not a graphical USB image, writable-data integration, physical test,
publisher/clone authentication, hot-unplug recovery, production MAC policy or
installer. Its fixed geometry deliberately differs from the authorized stick.
Carrier enforcing evidence does not establish image policy.

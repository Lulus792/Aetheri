# Separate audio carrier and administrative preflight

9 November 2026. This checkpoint prepares confined audio work. It does not enable
normal audio, update the HP image or finish the Aetheri desktop sandbox.

## Why a separate guest

The original guest has only 21,544,960 available root-filesystem bytes. Its
loaded policy export hashes to
`c836158721c0b314cea6e26ec8d18f71957b676180f049b3ca5a6957db9dceaf`;
the stored active policy hashes to
`66e742636c09c6ff2ee0cc507b9d36b17119964275f0c7184198cf645d5456`.
Different serialized bytes do not establish semantic differences. They also do
not justify reloading a copied store into a kernel with a quarantined runtime.
Keep the original running guest and policy untouched.

The new [audio lab](../../../spec/audio-lab.md) owns a separate 32 GiB qcow2 disk,
4 GiB RAM, two vCPUs, UEFI variables, SSH key and local AF_UNIX QMP endpoint.
It neither clones nor attaches the original disk. Its installer checks its own
UEFI/DMI/serial/geometry before partitioning. This is existing Fedora/QEMU test
infrastructure, not a new Aetheri dependency or an installer for physical disks.

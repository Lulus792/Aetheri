## Bound selection

The owned profile freezes `presentation.kind: aetheri-settings-v1`, its executable
path/digest, five presentation-source digests and a bounded raw CMake-cache digest.
The selected cache must identify the expected SDK prefix/package path and sanitizer
choice. The original SDK metadata, selected library and raw pin remain bound;
the unused upstream consumer executable is excluded. The own model has its own
two source digests rather than depending on the upstream example's copied model.
All eight runtime image entries are explicit. Different source sets need a new
kind/validator; completed records do not inspect current inputs or become another
presentation.

Root checks the selection before seat/history/start effects and passes its
canonical binding to the original user supervisor. That supervisor checks before
children and every fresh Settings spawn. Pin, presentation or model drift refuses
the request without a new process/fallback, while the desktop remains alive.
Exact restoration requires a fresh explicit request. Original process/pipe reuse,
retirement, launch limits and shutdown order are retained.

These are consistency checks over mutable developer inputs. Source/cache hashes
do not attest a reproducible build; actual mapped-image/library and matching
build/test evidence remain necessary. This does not supply production publisher
trust, sealed execution admission, normal app authorization or lease settlement.

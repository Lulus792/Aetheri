an unknown send error, preventing an automatic replacement error response.
Original obligations remain; a bounded local diagnostic records uncertainty.
Client timeout never implies rollback/drain. A delivered last reply likewise
cannot be revoked or interpreted as global readiness. The immutable fixed
helper still calls each phase once; no general replay/restart protocol is added.

bookkeeping; success is not confirmed supervisor-reference absence or lease
release. After admitted no-exec withdrawal, the original dead ProcessFD
can remain held until explicit local clear; a fresh controller cannot rebind it.
Do not reuse this no-exec proof for an executing starter.
